# PlantsHome privacy policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

PlantsHome is a plant care diary. This page explains what the app stores and why.

## What the app collects

| Data | Why | Where it is kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email address, password, display name | To create and sign in to your account | Firebase Authentication (Google). Passwords are handled by Firebase and never seen by the app. |
| Google account name and email (only if you choose "Continue with Google") | To sign you in | Firebase Authentication |
| Plant choices, daily care ticks, badges | To show your progress | On your phone only |
| Plant photos and your profile photo | Your photo album | On your phone only, in the app's private storage |

PlantsHome has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not sell or share your data.

## Feedback

The Feedback screen opens your own email app with your message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.

## Backups

If Android backup is on, your plant choices, progress and photos can be restored to a new phone. Your sign-in is not backed up, so you sign in again after a restore.

## Deleting your data

Profile → **Delete Account** removes your Firebase account and deletes your choices, progress and photos from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes everything stored on the phone.
